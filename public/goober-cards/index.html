How Goober Cards Works

Goal

  • Each side has a 3-Goober team.
  • First side to lose all 3 Goobers loses.
  • Online: both players choose actions, then Player 1 resolves.

Actions

  • Attack: +10% damage.
  • Defend: reduced incoming damage and more dodge.
  • Charge: +1 momentum and better power/special chance.
  • Trick: more dodge/score, less damage.
  • Swap: soften a hit while changing Goobers.

Momentum

  • Charge gives momentum.
  • Hits and dodges give momentum.
  • Knockouts give bonus momentum.
  • Ultimate costs 3 momentum online.

Numbers

Damage ≈ Attack × 1.35 - Defense × .68 + 6
Special ≈ 18 + Luck × .9
Dodge ≈ Speed × .75 + Luck × .55
