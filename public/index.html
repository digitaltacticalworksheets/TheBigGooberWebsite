What makes a Goober?

A Goober is a hand-drawn cartoon dog shaped like a loaf of bread, with no legs at all. The body stays simple and recognizable, while the personality changes with every costume, mood, theme, or strange idea.

  • Is a loaf (no legs)
  • Simple rounded body shape
  • Long snoot and big simple nose
  • Hand-drawn, imperfect lines
  • Named by style, costume, mood, or chaos level
